How to read this. Signal balance is tailwind signals minus headwind signals, divided by all signals. It runs from −1 (all headwinds) to +1 (all tailwinds); mixed signals count in the total. It measures how often a condition is described as adverse or favorable across companies, not the dollar size of the effect.

Method. Filings come from SEC EDGAR. The management discussion and risk factor sections are split into paragraphs, and Claude classifies each one into the topics above. Quotes are verbatim from the filings. Business location is the filer's principal business address on file with the SEC, which is its headquarters, not necessarily where its plants are.
