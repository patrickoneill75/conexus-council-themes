App visibility

Public apps are listed on the public grid at the site root. Hidden apps aren't listed there, but their page still works for anyone with the direct link. Admin-only apps aren't listed either, and their page itself redirects to sign-in unless you're already signed in as an admin -- same as this Settings page.
