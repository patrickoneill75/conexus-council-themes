How the employer tool works
  • Ranking: the original STARs model, unchanged -- Euclidean distance across 35 O*NET skill-importance scores. Smaller distance = closer occupational skill profile. This is what the table is sorted by.
  • Skill similarity: a 0-100% readout of that same Euclidean distance, scaled against the largest distance two occupations could possibly have. It moves in lockstep with the ranking, just in a more human-readable form.
  • Average wage increase: the percentage increase from the transfer occupation's Indiana annual wage to the target occupation's Indiana annual wage.
  • Wage matching: Indiana wage data use the BLS May 2019/2020 hybrid OEWS occupation structure -- matched by title/SOC first, then the official BLS hybrid crosswalk where needed.
  • Wage filter: when selected, results only include occupations with a lower Indiana annual median wage than the target occupation.
  • Skill gaps: directional -- skills more important in the target occupation than in the potential transfer occupation.
  • Gap %: the transfer occupation's shortfall relative to the target occupation's O*NET importance score: (target − source) / target.
  • Important: this is an occupation-level talent-pool screening tool, not an assessment of an individual applicant and not a substitute for required licenses, credentials, safety training, or employer-specific qualification.

How the worker tool works
  • Start with your current occupation: the model treats your current job as evidence of skills you may already have developed through work experience.
  • Ranking: potential next occupations are ranked using the same Euclidean distance across 35 O*NET skill-importance scores used in the employer tool.
  • Skill similarity: a 0-100% readout of that same Euclidean distance. Higher scores mean the potential occupation has a skill profile more similar to your current occupation, and the percentage always agrees with the table's sort order.
  • Average wage increase: the percentage increase from your current occupation's Indiana annual median wage to the potential occupation's Indiana annual median wage.
  • Wage filter: when selected, results only include occupations with a higher Indiana annual median wage than your current occupation.
  • Skills to build: clicking a potential occupation compares that target job with your current occupation and identifies the skills more important in the new job.
  • Gap %: your current occupation's skill-importance shortfall relative to the potential target occupation: (target − current) / target.
  • Important: this is an occupation-level career exploration tool. It does not assess an individual's actual proficiency, qualifications, education, licenses, or employer-specific requirements.
