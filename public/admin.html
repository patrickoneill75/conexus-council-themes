New Survey Upload

The raw survey export from the most recent meeting. Uploaded into the New Survey Directory, which feeds both the natural-language analysis and the quant dashboard.

Council Meeting Helper Upload

A fresh export of the Council Meeting Helper — it doesn't need to cover the full history, just whatever meetings it has. Rows are merged in by Meeting Date: anything not already on file gets added, and existing meetings are never touched or overwritten.

Update Dashboard

Claude reads the uploaded survey's free-response answers, extracts "areas to improve" items tagged to the existing category taxonomy, appends them to the Synthesis Data File, then publishes this quarter's top issues plus what's recurred quarter-over-quarter and year-over-year. In the same run, the quant dashboard is rebuilt from scratch out of every survey file in the New Survey Directory.

Refresh Dashboard

Rebuilds the quant dashboard from whatever's currently in Box — no new survey needed. Use this after adding, editing, or deleting files directly in Box, in either the Quant Data Folder or the New Survey Directory.
