How to read this. Signal balance = (tailwind signals − headwind signals) ÷ all signals, ranging from −1 (all headwinds) to +1 (all tailwinds); mixed signals stay in the denominator. It measures how often a condition is described as adverse or favourable across companies — not the dollar size of the effect.

Method. Filings are retrieved from SEC EDGAR, the MD&A and Risk Factors sections are extracted deterministically into a paragraph-level dataset, and each paragraph is classified by Claude into the topics above. Quotes are verbatim from the filings. "Business location" is the filer's principal business address on file with the SEC, which is its headquarters — not necessarily where its plants are.
