# Staffing and turnover

## Welder shortage

- Still can't fill three welder openings; posted for two months with few qualified applicants.
- The shortage is forcing more overtime onto the current crew.

## Overtime and burnout

- Overtime hours are up almost 30% quarter over quarter.
- Several people have mentioned looking for other jobs because of the schedule.

# Cost pressure

## Input materials

- Steel prices have been flat this quarter.
- If tariffs increase, we expect input costs to rise within a month or two.
